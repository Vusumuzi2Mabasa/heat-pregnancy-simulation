Heat pregnancy applied epidemiology workflow
All data are synthetic. Author Vusumuzi Mabasa.
1. Preserve original cohort and Bayesian results; audit identifiers, visits, timing, coding and exposure reconstruction.
2. Fit participant random intercept models for depression, vascular and anxiety outcomes; estimate mean differences per 5 degrees C.
3. Analyse the generic pregnancy complication with logistic regression and HC3 odds ratio confidence intervals; exclude postpartum measurements.
4. Diagnose model fit and functional forms; assess antenatal-only and within-person specifications.
5. Explore genotype and phase modification and an expression path decomposition with 300 participant bootstrap resamples.
6. Generate ggplot figures, gtsummary and flextable tables, README and Quarto Word manuscript.
7. Validate cohort counts, estimators and preservation hashes.
Run: Rscript --vanilla run_epidemiology.R --report
PDF on Windows: pwsh -File export_manuscript.ps1 -DocumentPath <absolute docx> -PdfPath <absolute pdf>
Full methodology, equations and limitations are in the README and Word manuscript.
